================================================================================
  NAVEZGANE HOSPITAL CHALLENGE
  A single-player survival challenge for 7 Days to Die 3.2
  by Frilioth  -  twitch.tv/Frilioth
================================================================================

You wake up in Navezgane Hospital, infected with Voss-7. It resists honey and
antibiotics, and nothing in the building will cure you. The only cure is at
Trader Jen's clinic, and the streets are overrun.

There is a wrecked gyrocopter on the roof. Eight parts will fly it.

You have about eight days.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  BEFORE YOU START - THE THINGS THAT ARE NOT LIKE VANILLA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Read these six. Every one of them has cost a playtester real hours.

  1. THE ENGINE IS NOT IN THE CARS.
     Wrecked vehicles give engines in the normal game. They do not here, no
     matter how long you wrench. An engine comes from a heavy loot bag, or you
     craft one at the workbench - which needs forged steel, and therefore the
     crucible.

  2. YOU CANNOT BUILD A WORKBENCH OR A CHEMISTRY STATION.
     There are several of each fixed around the hospital and you cannot craft
     your own, so learn where the nearest ones are.
     THE FORGE IS THE EXCEPTION - you can and should build one. It unlocks early
     in Forge Ahead, and you will need it, plus a crucible, to make the forged
     steel the engine recipe asks for.

  3. DO NOT LEAVE THE HOSPITAL GROUNDS.
     The car park is survivable. Past that, the infected are faster, angrier and
     do not lose interest. People who walk out do not walk back.

  4. DEATH IS PERMANENT.
     There is no second chance. If you die, the run is over.

  5. THE VIRUS ONLY EVER RISES.
     It starts at 5% and climbs about 1% every five minutes. At 100% you die.
     Suppressants push it back down while they are active, but never to zero,
     and when one runs out the climb resumes. They do not cure anything.

  6. PARTS COME FROM THE DEAD.
     Search every zombie you kill. Different types carry different parts.

Press ESC at any time in game for the full survival guide. Four tabs: what the
virus is and how it behaves, what each stage of it costs you, where all eight
parts come from, and some practical advice on staying alive.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  INSTALLING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Copy each folder into your 7 Days to Die "Mods" directory, e.g.

    ...\steamapps\common\7 Days To Die\Mods\

REQUIRED - the challenge will not work without these:

    Hospital                     the mod itself
    GyroRepairMod                builds and launches the gyrocopter
    FrilHospitalESCGuide         the ESC survival guide

The folder names above are just what the download ships as. You can rename any of
them if you like - the game identifies a mod by the Name in its ModInfo.xml, not
by its folder.

RECOMMENDED - included, and safe to leave out if you prefer:

    Frils-HUD                    custom HUD
    FrilRagdollFloorFix          stops bodies sinking through floors
    FrilHospitalMenuVideoCycler  rotating main-menu background videos

This mod contains code and must be enabled as such. EAC must be OFF.
Start the game, choose NEW GAME, and pick the Hospital Challenge group.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  SINGLE PLAYER ONLY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

THIS VERSION IS NOT SUPPORTED IN MULTIPLAYER. Please do not run it on a
dedicated server yet.

The challenge itself - the virus, the stages, the suppressants, permadeath - is
per-player and does work. But the rest of the mod assumes a single player, so on
a server the host gets a working game and everyone else does not.

A co-op version is planned.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  DIFFICULTY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Six presets, easiest first:

    Walk-In              ill, but not dying yet. For learning the hospital.
    Under Observation    stable for now. Forgiving, still expects attention.
    Critical             the challenge as intended. RECOMMENDED.
    Code Blue            deteriorating. Faster infected, heavier hits.
    Terminal             everything sprints. The roof is a long way up.
    Flatline             not expected to be survivable. Prove otherwise.

Critical is the default and the way the mod is meant to be played. Walk-In
exists so you can learn the building without it killing you - there is no shame
in starting there.

There is also an INCREASING INFECTION toggle on the Basic tab. Leave it ON for
the real challenge. Turned OFF, the virus is disabled entirely and your first
suppressant ends it for the whole run - it is there for players who want the
hospital without the clock. Runs with it off are marked as such and are not
ranked.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  IF YOU ESCAPE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Finishing writes a full debrief to

    Mods\<the Hospital Challenge folder>\stats\Hospital_Debrief.html

Open it in any browser. It has your timeline, your kills, your suppressant use,
your peak infection, and when you found each of the eight parts.

It also contains a LEADERBOARD CODE - a long string beginning HC-1-. It encodes
your run so it can be verified. Keep it. You can also print it in game at any
time with the console command:

    hospitalcode

The leaderboard is not live yet. Hold on to your code until it is.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  LANGUAGES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Polish, Brazilian Portuguese,
Russian, Turkish and Japanese.

Everything the mod adds is translated - items, objectives, the ESC guide, the
menu, the loading tips and the debrief messages. Place and character names
(Navezgane, Voss-7, Trader Jen) are left in English on purpose.

The translations outside English have not all been checked by native speakers
yet. If something reads badly in your language, please say so - corrections are
welcome and easy to apply.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  KNOWN ISSUES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - Multiplayer is not supported. See above.
  - The vanilla "Infection Chance" and "Infection Rate" sandbox options do
    nothing in this mod. Voss-7 replaces the vanilla infection entirely. They
    still appear in the options list because they are vanilla settings.
  - Loot respawns every 5 days. The hospital will feel picked clean before then.
    That is intended.

If you hit something else, a copy of your Player.log is far more useful than a
description. Thank you.
